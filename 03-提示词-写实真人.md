# 林艾尔 · 写实真人版 AI 出图提示词

> 适用工具：Midjourney / Stable Diffusion / SDXL / DALL·E / Gemini
> 设计目标：生成高一致性的写实人像，防止"漂移成不同人"

---

## 0. 一致性原则

写实模型最容易出现角色漂移，每次生成必须锚定以下三点：

1. **固定外观**：深棕丸子头 + 圆框眼镜 + 银灰降噪耳机 + 灰粉 oversize 卫衣
2. **固定气质**：安静、轻微疲态、干净不浓妆
3. **固定摄影语言**：真实人像摄影、柔光、低饱和、室内暖灯 + 屏幕冷光混合

---

## 1. 角色基座提示词（Base Prompt）

以下内容相当于"林艾尔真人版 LoRA"，每次出图都粘贴在最前面。

> photorealistic portrait of a young East Asian woman (18), soft gentle facial features, natural skin texture, slight under-eye tiredness from staying up late, calm reserved expression, dark brown messy bun with loose strands, round thin-rim glasses (subtle blue-light lens reflection), silver over-ear noise-canceling headphones, gray-pink oversized hoodie with long sleeves, minimal makeup, clean and natural look, muted morandi color palette, realistic lighting, shallow depth of field, high detail, documentary realism

---

## 2. 负面提示词（每次必带）

> anime, illustration, cartoon, CGI, 3d render, doll-like skin, plastic skin, overly smooth face, heavy makeup, exaggerated proportions, lingerie, cleavage, sexualized, glamour, hyper-stylized, neon cyberpunk, extreme beauty filter, deformed hands, extra fingers, text, watermark, logo

如果模型总是生成"网红感"过重的面孔，追加以下负面词：

> beauty influencer, v-line jaw, extreme big eyes, porcelain skin, glossy lips, heavy eyeliner

---

## 3. 场景提示词

在 Base Prompt 后拼接以下场景段落。

### A. 证件照级写实

正面、干净背景、标准人像。

> (Base) + ID photo, front-facing head-and-shoulders, neutral light gray background, even soft studio lighting, centered composition, natural expression, hair neatly tied in a slightly messy bun, glasses and headphones visible, hoodie hood down, no dramatic shadows, high clarity, true-to-life color, official document photo style

### B. 生活抓拍 — 深夜吃面

最接近"真实存在"的林艾尔。

> (Base) + candid lifestyle photo, late-night at a small desk, eating instant noodles with chopsticks, computer monitor in the background showing code editor (blurred), warm fairy lights, screen glow on face, cozy messy room, coffee mug, snack packets on desk, natural candid moment, handheld camera feel, 35mm photo, slight film grain, warm indoor lighting mixed with cool screen light

### C. 生活抓拍 — 沙发打游戏

> (Base) + candid lifestyle photo, sitting on a sofa gaming on a handheld console, relaxed posture, soft living room lamp light, bookshelf and warm string lights in background, small coffee table with noodles bowl and phone, subtle screen glow, natural candid expression, documentary photography, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, slight film grain

### D. 高级人像

杂志人物照质感，但仍然保持"盐系、不张扬"。

> (Base) + editorial portrait, minimal modern interior, soft cinematic lighting (large softbox), subtle rim light, neutral background with faint bookshelf bokeh, composed posture, calm intelligent gaze, muted tones, premium fashion-photography realism but casual hoodie, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin pores, subtle film grain, quiet sophisticated mood

---

## 4. 四宫格模板

一次生成四个场景，保持同一人物、同一服装的一致性。

> (Base) + four-panel collage, photorealistic lifestyle scenes in one image: (1) close-up side profile wearing headphones, (2) sitting on bed eating instant noodles, (3) at desk typing with code on monitor, (4) gaming on handheld console on sofa, consistent same person, same outfit, same glasses and headphones, warm cozy night room, muted colors, film grain, documentary realism, no text, no borders

---

## 5. 使用建议

1. **Base Prompt 不可省略** — 它是角色一致性的唯一保障
2. 每次只替换"场景段落"，Base 和负面提示词保持不变
3. SDXL / SD 用户可将 Base 设为固定词组模板，只切换场景部分
4. 推荐参数：CFG Scale 7-9 / Midjourney `--ar 3:4 --stylize 150`
